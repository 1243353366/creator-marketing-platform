Your paywall, your transactions (we never touch them)

You set your own checkout link for whatever you market — content, products, services, courses, anything. If you sell unrestricted goods, standard processors (Stripe, PayPal business, Cash App business profile) are usually fine — check each provider's terms. If you market adult content, use a processor whose policy explicitly permits it (Segpay, CCBill, Verotel candidates); Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, and Stripe prohibit or restrict it. A purchase relabeled as a "tip" is still a commercial transaction. We do not run a tipping feature and never hold your money. Full options and policy citations: docs/PAYMENTS.md.
